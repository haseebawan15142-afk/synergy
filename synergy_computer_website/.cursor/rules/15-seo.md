# SEO

## Fundamentals

- Unique, descriptive `<title>` and meta description per page.
- One H1 aligned with primary keyword intent; natural language, not stuffing.

## URLs

- Readable slugs (`/services/business-it-support`), lowercase, hyphens.
- Canonical URLs when duplicate content exists.

## Structured Data

- JSON-LD for Organization, LocalBusiness (if applicable), Product/Offer where accurate.
- Validate with Google Rich Results Test after changes.

## Technical

- Valid HTML; crawlable links (real `<a href>` for navigation).
- `robots.txt` and sitemap maintained; noindex only for staging or thin/duplicate pages intentionally.

## Open Graph & Social

- og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630 recommended) on shareable pages.

## Content

- Internal links between related products/services.
- Fresh, accurate NAP (name, address, phone) on contact pages for local SEO.
