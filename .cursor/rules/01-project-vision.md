# Project Vision

## Purpose

Synergy Computer website: a professional, fast, trustworthy digital presence for products, services, and support.

## Goals

- Communicate value clearly to retail and business customers.
- Drive inquiries, store visits, and online engagement.
- Reflect reliability, expertise, and modern technology.

## Audience

- Home and small-business buyers seeking computers and accessories.
- IT decision-makers evaluating bulk or service offerings.
- Existing customers looking for support, warranty, and contact info.

## Success Criteria

- Consistent brand and messaging across all pages.
- Mobile-first experience with strong Core Web Vitals.
- Content that is accurate, scannable, and easy to maintain.

## Out of Scope (Unless Requested)

- E-commerce checkout flows not defined in product requirements.
- Features that bypass established design, accessibility, and performance rules.
