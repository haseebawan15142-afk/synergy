# Design Principles

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity** — One primary action per section; hierarchy guides the eye.
2. **Consistency** — Reuse spacing, type scale, and components from the design system.
3. **Trust** — Professional typography, real imagery, accurate specs and pricing disclaimers.
4. **Efficiency** — Minimal steps to find products, services, and contact options.
5. **Accessibility** — Design for keyboard, screen readers, and reduced motion from the start.

## Visual Tone

- **Reference site (layout/UX only):** [e360.com](https://www.e360.com/) — see `.cursor/docs/design-reference-e360.md`.
- Clean, enterprise B2B; section-based homepage, strong CTAs, partner and industry grids.
- **70% white/off-white** backgrounds for an open, enterprise feel.
- **20% black/gray** for type, borders, and layout structure.
- **10% Synergy Green** (`#357C3C`) only for CTAs, links, icons, and active states.
- Generous whitespace; avoid dense walls of text.

## Do

- Align content to a grid; keep line length readable (~60–75 characters for body).
- Use established breakpoints and container widths.
- Pair headlines with supporting copy that answers “why this matters.”

## Don’t

- Introduce one-off layouts that break the grid.
- Rely on color alone to convey meaning.
- Hide critical info (price, availability, contact) behind unnecessary interaction.
